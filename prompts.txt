## The Problem Explained

Your analysis is correct. The issue is with the asymmetric address vector setup in EFA-direct mode:

Current Implementation:
• Only the sender calls addPeerAddress() to insert the receiver's address into its AV
• The receiver never inserts the sender's address into its AV

Why this works in regular EFA mode:
• EFA provider handles bidirectional communication through wire protocols
• ACKs can be sent back even without explicit AV setup on receiver side

Why this fails in EFA-direct mode:
• EFA-direct bypasses wire protocols for performance
• **Delivery-complete semantics** require hardware-level ACKs
• Receiver cannot send ACKs back to sender without sender's address in its AV
• Send completions hang indefinitely waiting for ACKs that never arrive
